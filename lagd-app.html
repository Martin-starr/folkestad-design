Snap-scroll — one section at a time
The intro curtain plays in Preview and in the exported site — not while editing. Section-to-section transitions live on each section, under “Scroll transition”. Snap-scroll turns the whole site into slot-machine sections — wheel / arrow keys / swipe advance one at a time. Best with 3–8 sections.

1× is the built-in feel. Drag toward 0 for subtle, toward 2 for strong. Greyed rows need their effect switched on above.
